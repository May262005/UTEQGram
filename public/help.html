Help

Welcome to UTEQGram! This is a social media platform where you can share your photos and connect with others. Here are some tips to get started:

  • To create a post, click on the "Create Post" button and fill in the title and location.
  • You can enable notifications to stay updated on new posts and interactions.
  • Explore the feed to see posts from other users and engage with their content.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact our support team.
